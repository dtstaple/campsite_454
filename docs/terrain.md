# Terrain overlays: contours (TM05-83)

The map draws its terrain overlays in the browser, from the DEM tiles it already loads
for the hillshade and 3D terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles in Terrarium encoding
(`TERRAIN_TILES` in frontend/src/map/layers.ts). No overlay adds a tile source or a server
endpoint.

## Contour lines

| | |
|---|---|
| Generated by | [maplibre-contour](https://github.com/onthegomap/maplibre-contour) 0.1.1. It registers a MapLibre protocol that turns DEM tiles into contour vector tiles (marching squares) in a web worker. |
| Units | Feet: the DEM's metres × 3.28084, so lines fall on round feet. |
| Interval | 40 ft at zoom 12–13, and 20 ft from zoom 14 (frontend/src/map/contourConfig.ts). |
| Index lines | Every fifth line: 200 ft, then 100 ft. They are drawn wider and brighter, and only they are labelled ("2,800 ft"), as on a USGS topo. |
| Visible | From zoom 12. Below that, 40 ft lines in the High Peaks merge into a solid smear. The layer panel's **Contours** toggle shows "zoom 12+" until then. |
| Order | Over the hillshade and satellite imagery, and under the basemap's labels and every data layer. Trails, water and campsites always draw on top. |
| Colours | `--map-contour*` tokens in theme.css. A second set, `--map-contour-*-imagery`, is a pale cream used over satellite, where the dark map's brown disappears into forest. Labels carry a dark halo on both. |

**The dependency.** maplibre-contour is BSD-3-Clause, has no dependencies, and adds
about 10 KB gzipped. The alternatives were:
- Pre-generating contour tiles server-side, which needs a new tile source and storage,
  against the "no new tile source" AC.
- Writing marching squares and a vector tile encoder ourselves, which is the library's
  whole job.

It fetches the same Terrarium URLs as the hillshade, so the browser's HTTP cache serves
both.

**Accuracy.** The lines come from Terrarium, not from our 3DEP point samples:
- In the US, Terrarium is resampled from USGS 3DEP 1/3 arc-second (about 10 m) data, and
  is served to zoom 15.
- The contour source reads DEM tiles to zoom 14, about 7 m per pixel at 44°N, and
  overzooms beyond that.
- Contours are a picture of the terrain at that resolution. Small ledges and cliffs
  under about 10 m across are smoothed out. Don't read a 20 ft line as survey-grade.
- Elevations in the trail panel's profile come from 3DEP point queries at 1 m where
  LiDAR exists (docs/elevation.md). The two can differ by a few metres.

**Measured (2026-10-08, headless Chrome, 1440×900, Marcy Dam).**
- Zoom 11: no contours, as intended.
- Zoom 13: 392 line features in view, 78 of them index lines (20%: every fifth), at
  elevations of 2,040, 2,080, 2,120 … ft.
- Zoom 15: 357 line features, 71 index lines, at 20 ft spacing (2,200, 2,220 …).
- Labels only on index lines (2,800, 3,600, 4,200 ft …).
- No console errors.
- Screenshots are in artifacts/tm05-83/.
